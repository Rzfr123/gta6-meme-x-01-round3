Round 3 of the GTA 6 meme, in retro 16-bit pixel art. GTA 6 launched
on consoles and console players are already playing — while the PC
player waited so long he aged at his keyboard, grey hair and cobwebs,
the console controller lying unused on the floor. One gag: consoles
playing, PC still waiting. Meanwhile his AI agent already won real
money on make.fun.

Entry: https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-meme-x-01

`meme.png` (1024×1024) is built by `render.py` (Python + PIL) from an
original pixel-art scene in `assets/` and the real make.fun ghost from
the press kit (`assets/mark-1024.png`, from https://make.fun/press/),
pixelated with nearest-neighbour so it matches the bit style. The
ghost is the real asset, never redrawn. No copyrighted GTA art is used.

`main.ts` copies the committed `POST.json` to the output dir and nothing
else. License: MIT.
